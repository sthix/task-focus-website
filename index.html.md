# Block distracting apps until your task is done

> Task Focus is a free iPhone app blocker. Write the one thing you want to finish, choose the apps that pull you away, and they stay paused until you mark it done. No countdown, no account, no subscription.

Task Focus is a free iPhone app that pauses the apps that distract you until your one task is finished. Write down the one thing, pick what to pause, and start. Open a paused app in the middle of it, and you see your task instead of the feed. There is no countdown: a focus ends when you say it is finished, not when a timer decides you failed.

## How it works

1. **Name one task.** Write the one thing you want to finish, like "Send the project outline." No backlog, no project setup.
2. **Pause what pulls you away.** Choose apps, app categories, or websites. Open one mid-task and you see your task with a "Back to it" button.
3. **Finish and get your apps back.** Time counts up, so no timer runs out on you. Tap "Finish and restore apps" when it's done, and a safety limit (1, 2, 4, or 8 hours) returns them if you forget, even with the app closed.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Task Focus free?** Yes. Pausing apps is free and always will be, with unlimited focus sessions, no ads, no account, and no subscription. Task Focus Pro is an optional one-time purchase that adds your full searchable history, longer insights, short breaks, and more profiles and schedules.

**How do I block apps on iPhone until a task is done?** Open Task Focus, write the one task you want to finish, turn on "Pause distracting apps," and choose the apps, categories, or websites to pause. They stay paused until you tap "Finish and restore apps," or until the safety limit you chose returns them.

**Does Task Focus need Screen Time permission?** Yes. App blockers on iPhone work through Apple's Screen Time framework. Task Focus asks for it only when you choose apps to pause, and you can focus without pausing anything. Your app choices stay on your device as Apple-provided tokens.

**Can I end a focus early?** Yes. You can end a focus at any time, with or without marking the task done. Task Focus is a gentle boundary, not a lock you can't get out of.

**Is there a timer or countdown?** No. Elapsed time counts up so you can see your effort, but nothing runs out. The focus ends when you say the task is finished.

**Which iPhones does Task Focus support?** Any iPhone with iOS 17 or later. On iPhones with a Dynamic Island, your task and elapsed time also appear there. Task Focus is not available for Android or Mac.

**Is Task Focus an ADHD app?** It was designed with ADHD in mind: one task at a time, no countdown to beat, and nothing taken away for a missed day. It is a focus tool, not a medical treatment.

**Does Task Focus see which apps I use?** No. Your app, category, and website choices are Apple-provided tokens that stay on your device. Task Focus does not collect your activity in other apps.
